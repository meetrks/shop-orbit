Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how {{ site.legal_name }} ("{{ site.domain }}", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects the personal information of customers who use this website. By creating an account or otherwise using the site, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: full name, email address, phone number, and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text).
  • Shipping and profile information: address lines, city, state, postal code, and country, provided when you update your profile or place an order.
  • Order information: the products, quantities, and prices in each order, and any optional delivery note you provide at checkout.
  • Product information: product listings, descriptions, prices, stock levels, and images, including source photos and generated variants processed through the PicWeight tool.
  • Technical information: standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps) and session/cookie data required to keep you logged in and your cart intact between visits.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To process, fulfil, and communicate with you about your orders, including verifying payments through our payment gateway.
  • To maintain your shopping cart and order history across sessions.
  • To operate internal tools such as product listing management and the PicWeight image studio.
  • To respond to support requests and enforce our Terms & Conditions.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the site, including detecting and preventing fraud or abuse.

4. Payment Data

Payments are processed by Razorpay, our third-party payment gateway. We do not collect, process, or store your card numbers, bank account details, UPI PIN, or other payment credentials — those are entered directly into Razorpay's secure checkout and never touch our servers. We retain only the payment outcome (success/failure), the amount, the payment method used (e.g. UPI, card), and gateway-issued transaction identifiers, used to confirm your order and for refunds and support.

5. Image Uploads and PicWeight

Product photos processed through the PicWeight image studio are stored on our servers and processed automatically (cropping, background centering, and border/zoom variant generation) to produce clean, consistent listing photography. These images are visible to visitors browsing the linked product listing. We do not sell or share these images with third parties outside of normal site operation.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with service providers who help us operate the site (for example, hosting and infrastructure providers), and only to the extent necessary for them to provide that service, or when required to comply with applicable law, legal process, or a valid government request.

7. Data Retention

We retain account, order, and listing data for as long as your account remains active and for a reasonable period afterward to comply with legal, accounting, and dispute-resolution obligations. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time, subject to records we are legally required to retain (such as order history for tax purposes).

8. Cookies and Sessions

We use strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in, protect against cross-site request forgery, and remember your cart between page loads. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Your Rights

You can review and update your profile and shipping information at any time from your account page. You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or request that we correct or delete it, by contacting us using the details below.

10. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards — including password hashing, access controls, and HTTPS in production — to protect your information. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted to or stored on this site.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
