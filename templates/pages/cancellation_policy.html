Cancellation Policy

Last updated: {% now "d F Y" %}

1. When You Can Cancel

You can cancel an order yourself, from your order page, at any point while it's:

  • Awaiting payment
  • Showing a failed payment
  • Paid but not yet shipped

Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled through your account — at that point, our Return & Refund Policy applies instead once the item is delivered.

2. Refunds on Cancellation

Cancelling an order that hasn't been paid for doesn't involve a charge or refund. If you cancel an order that has already been paid, the cancellation itself doesn't trigger an automatic refund — our team reviews the cancelled order and processes the refund to your original payment method shortly after.

3. Cancelling a Return Request

If you've raised a return request but change your mind, you can cancel it yourself from your order page as long as it's still in "Requested" status — once our team has approved or rejected it, cancellation is no longer self-service; contact us instead.

4. Contact Us

Questions about cancelling an order can be directed to us via our contact page{% if site.company_email %} or by emailing {{ site.company_email }}{% endif %}.
