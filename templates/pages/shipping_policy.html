Shipping Policy

Last updated: {% now "d F Y" %}

1. Dispatch

Orders are dispatched once payment is confirmed, after a quality check of the item(s). You'll receive an email as soon as your order ships, along with the carrier and tracking details — the same information is always visible on your order page.

2. Delivery Charges

Any delivery/handling charge for an item is shown as part of its listed price at checkout — we don't add surprise shipping fees at the cart or payment step.

3. Delivery Estimates

Delivery timelines depend on your location and the courier partner assigned to your order, and are estimates, not guarantees — factors like weather, regional logistics disruptions, or courier delays can affect them. You can track real-time progress using the tracking link/number provided once your order ships.

4. Serviceable Areas

We currently ship across serviceable pincodes within India. If your pincode isn't serviceable, this will be flagged before you complete checkout. If you believe your area should be serviceable but isn't, please contact us.

5. Order Tracking

Once your order ships, its carrier, tracking number, and tracking link are available on your order page and included in your shipping confirmation email. Status updates (in transit, out for delivery, delivered) are reflected there as they happen.

6. Delivery Issues

If a delivery attempt fails, is delayed well beyond the estimate, or your package arrives damaged, please contact us with your order number as soon as possible so we can follow up with the courier on your behalf.

7. Related Policies

For cancelling an order before it ships, see our Cancellation Policy. For returning a delivered item, see our Return & Refund Policy.

8. Contact Us

Questions about shipping can be directed to us via our contact page{% if site.company_email %} or by emailing {{ site.company_email }}{% endif %}.
