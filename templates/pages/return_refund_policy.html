Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: {% now "d F Y" %}

1. Return Window

You can request a return within {{ return_window_days }} days of your order being marked delivered. Requests made after this window has closed can't be raised through your account — please contact us if you have an exceptional circumstance.

2. Eligible Reasons

You can request a return for any of the following reasons, from your order page:

  • The item is defective or arrived damaged
  • You received the wrong item
  • The item is not as described
  • You no longer need the item

Eligibility ultimately depends on the item's condition on inspection and is reviewed case-by-case, consistent with applicable consumer protection law.

3. How to Request a Return

  1. Open the order from your account and select "Request Return."
  2. Choose the reason and describe the issue.
  3. Our team reviews the request and approves or rejects it, with a reason if rejected.
  4. Once approved, ship the item back and enter the tracking details from your order page.
  5. We inspect the item on receipt and initiate your refund.

4. Refunds

Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method via Razorpay. Refunds are typically initiated within 2–3 business days of the returned item being received and inspected, and can take a further 5–7 business days to reflect in your account depending on your bank or payment provider. You'll receive an email confirmation once your refund is processed.

5. Non-Returnable Items

Items that are visibly used, altered, or missing their original packaging/tags may not qualify for return. If a specific product isn't eligible for return, this is noted on its product page.

6. Related Policies

To cancel an order before it ships, see our Cancellation Policy. For delivery timelines and tracking, see our Shipping Policy.

7. Contact Us

Questions about a return or refund can be directed to us via our contact page{% if site.company_email %} or by emailing {{ site.company_email }}{% endif %}.
